The Settlement Challenge

Korea Needs Global Talent. Settling In Shouldn't Be Hard.

Korea is opening wider doors to foreign students and workers. Yet, the critical first 90 days of relocation remain filled with language barriers, unfamiliar regulations, and fragmented services.

Language & Contract Confusion

Housing leases, telecom agreements, and administrative forms are written in complex Korean legal terms without reliable translation.

Scattered, Unverified Services

Newcomers have to wander between unverified brokers, individual shops, and forums with no single trusted partner to rely on.

Lack of Personalized Care

Every resident has different needs based on nationality, visa type, budget, and school/workplace. Generalized FAQs rarely help in real life.
